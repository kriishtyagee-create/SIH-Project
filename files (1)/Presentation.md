# AYUSH SkillBridge — SIH 2026 Presentation

**Problem Statement Title:** Portal for Academia–Industry Collaboration for Skill Mapping, Internships and Placements
**Theme:** Miscellaneous · **Category:** Software

## About the idea

AYUSH SkillBridge is an AI-powered platform that brings students, colleges, and industries onto one portal. It maps student skills against industry needs, flags gaps, recommends targeted courses, and surfaces relevant internship and placement opportunities — turning a fragmented, manual process into a single data-driven pipeline.

**Core capabilities:**
- AI-based skill gap analysis matched against real industry demand
- Personalized learning and certification recommendations
- Smart internship/job matching with an AI match score
- Role-based access for students, faculty, industry partners, and admins
- Analytics dashboard for institutions to track placement readiness

**Tech stack:** React.js frontend · Node.js/Express REST APIs · PostgreSQL/MySQL · Python/NLP for skill matching · cloud-based deployment for national scalability.
